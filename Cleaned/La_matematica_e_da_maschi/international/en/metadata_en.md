## Title
Is math a boys' thing?

## YouTube description

The study "Implicit Stereotypes: Evidence from Teachers' Gender Bias" by Michela Carlana (The Quarterly Journal of Economics, 2019) uses an implicit association test to measure how strongly middle-school teachers in Northern Italy link math with boys and the humanities with girls. When girls are assigned a math teacher with stronger stereotypes, the math gap with boys widens. Girls become less confident in their own abilities and, following their teachers' guidance, more often choose less demanding high schools. Literature teachers' stereotypes, by contrast, have no clear effect.
