# Metadati Video - Do Runoff Elections Keep Extremists in Check? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Moderating Political Extremism Single Round versus Runoff Elections under Plurality Rule" by Bordignon, Nannicini, Tabellini, published in the American Economic Review in 2016. Can the electoral system really save politics from extremism? This study demonstrates how runoff voting strips radical parties of their leverage compared to single-round elections, fostering more moderate policy choices. Analyzing data from Italian municipalities, these economists reveal the concrete impact of voting systems on the stability of our cities.
