# Metadati Video - Did the Potato Make the World Grow? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "The Potato's Contribution to Population and Urbanization Evidence from a Historical Experiment" by Nunn, Qian, published in The Quarterly Journal of Economics in 2011. How did a simple tuber change the course of human history forever? This study reveals how the introduction of the potato to the Old World literally unleashed a population and urban boom between the 18th and 19th centuries. Let's discover the incredible economic and social impact of this silent food revolution.
