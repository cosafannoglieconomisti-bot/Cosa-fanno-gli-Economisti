# Metadati Video - Do Persecutions Leave Scars? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "The Long Run Effects of Religious Persecution: Evidence from the Spanish Inquisition" by Mauricio Drelichman, Jordi Vidal-Robert, and Hans-Joachim Voth, a 2021 working paper, analyzes the very long-run effects of religious persecution in Spain and shows that the areas hit most intensely by the Inquisition still display lower income, lower education, and lower social trust today. Using a new database with more than 67,000 trials between 1480 and 1820, the paper reconstructs with geographic precision where repression was strongest and documents how those historical wounds still shape economic outcomes today.
