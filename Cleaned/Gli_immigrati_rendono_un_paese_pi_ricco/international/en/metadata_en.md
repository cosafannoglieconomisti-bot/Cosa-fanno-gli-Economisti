# Metadati Video - Do Immigrants Make a Country Richer? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
Do immigrants harm or enrich the economy in the long run? The study "Immigrants and the Making of America" by Sequeira, Nunn, Qian, published in the Review of Economic Studies in 2020, addresses this question. This groundbreaking study analyzes the impact of European mass migration to the United States between 1850 and 1920. The findings demonstrate that areas which welcomed more immigrants back then are significantly richer, more educated, and more industrialized today.
