# Metadati Video - Political Views: Blame Your Friends? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Friendship Networks and Political Opinions" by Algan, Dalvit, Do, Le Chapelain, Zenou, published in the American Economic Review in 2026. Can our friendships really change the way we vote? This study leverages a natural experiment at the prestigious university Sciences Po to analyze the impact of social networks on political opinions. The results show that interaction reduces ideological distances by 44%, revealing the hidden mechanisms behind polarization.
