# Metadati Video - Is Wealth Deserved? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
Why do two countries with the same level of inequality choose opposite fiscal paths? The study "The Evolution of Ideology, Fairness and Redistribution" by Alesina, Cozzi, Mantovan, published in The Economic Journal in 2012, explores this very question. This study reveals how our historical ideas of 'merit' and 'luck' influence taxation and economic growth across generations. Discover how our perception of social justice shapes our wealth.
