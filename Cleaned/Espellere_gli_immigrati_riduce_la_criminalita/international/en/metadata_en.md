# Metadati Video - Does Deporting Immigrants Reduce Crime? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
Based on the study "Immigration Enforcement and Crime" by Pinotti, published in the American Economic Review: Papers & Proceedings in 2015, does deporting undocumented immigrants really reduce crime? This study analyzes the impact of deportation policies in Italy, revealing how the lack of legal status drives individuals toward crime, but also how bilateral expulsion agreements manage to mitigate this phenomenon. A crucial economic perspective for understanding security and integration.
