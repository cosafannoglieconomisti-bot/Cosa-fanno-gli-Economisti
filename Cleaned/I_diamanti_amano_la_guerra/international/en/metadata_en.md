# Metadati Video - Do Diamonds Love War? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Diamonds Are Forever, Wars Are Not: Is Conflict Bad for Private Firms?" by Guidolin and La Ferrara, published in the American Economic Review in 2007, reveals a surprising perspective: war is not always bad for all businesses. By analyzing the civil war in Angola and the diamond market, we discover that the end of conflict can actually be perceived as bad news by investors. It seems that war can create unexpected advantages—such as lower operating costs or barriers to entry—that outweigh its dangers.
