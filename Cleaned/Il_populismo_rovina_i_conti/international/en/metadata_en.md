## YouTube Description

The study "A Costly Commitment: Populism, Economic Performance, and the Quality of Bureaucracy" by Luca Bellodi, Massimo Morelli and Matia Vannoni, published in the American Journal of Political Science in 2024, measures what happens to public finances and bureaucracy when a populist mayor wins: across more than 8,000 Italian municipalities and over 20 years of data, an RDD on close elections shows fewer debt repayments, more contracts with cost overruns, higher managerial turnover (especially forced exits) and a clear drop in the share of bureaucrats with postgraduate degrees — populists push "unconditional" policy commitments even against budget constraints and expert assessment.
