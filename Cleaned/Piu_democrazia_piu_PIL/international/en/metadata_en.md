# Metadati Video - More Democracy, More GDP? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
Based on the study "Democracy Does Cause Growth" by Acemoglu, Naidu, Restrepo, Robinson, published in the Journal of Political Economy in 2019: Does democracy really benefit the economy, or is it just a myth? This landmark study debunks those who point to autocracies like China as models of efficiency, proving that countries that democratize achieve 20% higher GDP in the long run. Let's discover how political freedom translates concretely into social well-being.
