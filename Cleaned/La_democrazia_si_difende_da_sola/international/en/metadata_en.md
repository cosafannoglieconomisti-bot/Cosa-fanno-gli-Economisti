# Metadati Video - Does Democracy Defend Itself? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Successful Democracies Breed Their Own Support" by Acemoglu, Ajzenman, Aksoy, Fiszbein, Molina, published in Review of Economic Studies in 2024. Is democracy really in crisis, or does it just need to prove its worth? This study reveals that citizens support democratic institutions only if they deliver economic growth, public services, and a real fight against corruption. Ensuring state efficiency is the only true antidote to authoritarianism.
