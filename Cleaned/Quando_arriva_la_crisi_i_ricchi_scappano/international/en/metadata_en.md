# Video Metadata - When a crisis hits, do the rich run away? (EN)

## Description
The study "Knockin' on H(e)aven's door. Financial crises and offshore wealth" by Silvia Marchesi and Giovanna Marcolongo (Journal of International Economics, 2025) looks at 144 developing countries over more than forty years and asks where the money goes when a banking or public debt crisis breaks out. The answer: to tax havens. A few years after the start of the crisis, deposits in tax havens grow by almost a third, while nothing happens to flows towards other foreign countries. It is not an escape from taxes: the main driver is the fear of expropriation, and the phenomenon mostly concerns countries with weak institutions. So tax havens, besides fostering tax evasion and corruption in normal times, soak up resources exactly when they are needed most.
