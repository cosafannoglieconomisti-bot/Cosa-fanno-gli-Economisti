# Metadati Video - Is Corruption Ever Forgotten? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Never Forget the First Time: The Persistent Effects of Corruption and the Rise of Populism in Italy" by Gianmarco Daniele, Arnstein Aassve and Marco Le Moglie, published in The Journal of Politics in 2023, analyzes the long-term effects of the Mani Pulite scandal on trust in institutions and shows that young people exposed to the corruption crisis of the early 1990s are still more likely today to support populist parties.
