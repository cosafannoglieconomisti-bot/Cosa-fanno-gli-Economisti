# Metadati Video - Less Work, More Domestic Violence? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Job Displacement, Unemployment Benefits and Domestic Violence" by Bhalotra, Britto, Pinotti, Sampaio, published in the Review of Economic Studies in 2025: What happens when you lose your job? Beyond the financial toll, a shocking new study reveals a dramatic link to the rise in domestic violence. Analyzing data from Brazil, the researchers show how unemployment and, paradoxically, even government benefits can increase the risks for women.
