# Metadati Video - Should Women Be Taxed Less? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Gender-Based Taxation and the Division of Family Chores" by Alesina, Ichino, Karabarbounis, published in American Economic Journal: Economic Policy in 2011, explores a provocative question: Should women be taxed less? Is it just a provocation or a valid economic strategy? According to optimal taxation theory, women have a more elastic labor supply, suggesting lower tax rates. Yet, reality is often the opposite, and this proposal is largely ignored. Let's discover why.
