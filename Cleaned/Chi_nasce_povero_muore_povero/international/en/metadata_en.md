# Metadati Video - Are Those Born Poor Destined to Die Poor? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Intergenerational Mobility and Preferences for Redistribution" by Alesina, Stantcheva, Teso, published in the American Economic Review in 2018 asks: Are those born poor destined to remain poor? This groundbreaking study reveals how the perception of social mobility influences our ideas about wealth redistribution, uncovering the gap between the American Dream and European reality. You will discover why the political left and right have opposing views on the role of the state in correcting inequalities.
