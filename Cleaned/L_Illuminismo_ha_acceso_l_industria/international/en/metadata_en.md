# Video Metadata - L'Illuminismo ha acceso l'industria? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube

The study "Human Capital and Industrialization: Evidence from the Age of Enlightenment" by Mara P. Squicciarini and Nico Voigtländer, published in The Quarterly Journal of Economics in 2015, analyzes whether industrial growth depends only on average literacy or also on the presence of local elites with scientific and technical knowledge. Using subscriptions to the Encyclopédie in eighteenth-century France, the paper shows that cities with a greater density of advanced knowledge grew more after the start of industrialization.
