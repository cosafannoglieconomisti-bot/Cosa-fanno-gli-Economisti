# Video metadata - Does the union break gangmastering?

## YouTube description

The article "When Migrants Mobilize against Labor Exploitation: Evidence from the Italian Farmlands" by Gemma Dipoppa (American Political Science Review, 2025) studies an agricultural-union campaign in Italian fields: information on rights, legal aid, and incentives to report gangmasters, including a humanitarian residence permit for victims of exploitation. After the intervention, in the municipalities reached, reports and news about labor racketeering rise: the chance of at least one news story increases by 15%. Seizures of assets from criminal organizations also increase. Locals become less distrustful of migrants and more favorable toward immigration. Showing farmworkers' vulnerability, instead of triggering backlash, can strengthen solidarity.
