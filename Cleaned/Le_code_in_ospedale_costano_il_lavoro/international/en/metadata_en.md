# Metadati Video - Do Hospital Queues Cost Jobs? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Hospital Queues, Patient Health, and Labor Supply" by Godøy, Haaland, Huitfeldt, Votruba, published in American Economic Journal: Economic Policy in 2024. When healthcare keeps you waiting, it's not just your health that suffers – your job does too! A study from Norway reveals that long waits for orthopedic surgeries do not lead to increased use of other healthcare services, but result in a drastic reduction in labor market participation. Specifically, individuals already on sick leave when requesting treatment risk becoming trapped in permanent disability, with lasting economic consequences.
