# Metadati Video - Does Justice Have a Color? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Test_of_Racial_Bias_in_Capital_Sentencing" by Alesina, La Ferrara, published in the American Economic Review in 2014. Is the death penalty in the US truly influenced by racism? Economists Alesina and La Ferrara devised an ingenious method to prove it, analyzing how many death sentences are overturned on appeal. The results reveal a clear racial bias, especially in the Southern States.
