# Metadati Video - Is politics more risky for women? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The article "Attacking Women or their Policies? Understanding Violence against Women in Politics" by Gianmarco Daniele, Gemma Dipoppa, and Massimo Pulejo (The Journal of Politics, forthcoming) shows that female mayors are more often targets of political violence. Over fourteen years of attacks collected by Avviso Pubblico, narrowly electing a woman increases the probability of experiencing at least one attack from 8.6% to 22.8%, almost three times. This does not depend on different policies. After an attack, female mayors run for re-election less.
