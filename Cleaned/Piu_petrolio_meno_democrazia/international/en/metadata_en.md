# Video metadata - More oil, less democracy?

## Title
More oil, less democracy?

## YouTube description

The study "Do Natural Resources Influence Who Comes to Power, and How?" by Maria Carreri and Oeindrila Dube (The Journal of Politics, 2017) looks at Colombia, a country scarred by civil war, and asks whether oil changes who comes to power. When the price of oil rises, politicians linked to right-wing paramilitary groups win more often in oil-producing municipalities. At the same time paramilitary violence increases and competition shrinks: fewer candidates run and winners take office by wider margins. In the end, fewer centrist politicians are elected. Natural resources shape not only how leaders govern, but also how they are chosen.
