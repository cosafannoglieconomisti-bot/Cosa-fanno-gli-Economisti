# Metadati Video - Who Really Becomes a Politician (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
Based on the study "Who Becomes a Politician" by Dal Bó, Finan, Folke, Persson, Rickne, published in The Quarterly Journal of Economics in 2017. Are politicians actually less intelligent than the rest of us, or is it just a myth? This groundbreaking study analyzes the entire Swedish political class, revealing that leaders are not only more competent than average, but also faithfully represent every social class. Discover a surprising 'inclusive meritocracy'.
