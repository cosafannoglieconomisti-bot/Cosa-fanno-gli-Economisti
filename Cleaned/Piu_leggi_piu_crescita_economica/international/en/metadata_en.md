# Video Metadata - More laws, more economic growth? (EN)

## YouTube Description
This working paper by Elliott Ash, Massimo Morelli, and Matia Vannoni, dated June 5, 2024, studies whether greater legislative production can support economic growth in the United States. Using U.S. state laws from 1965 to 2012, natural language tools, and a shift-share design, the authors find that, at the margin, more legislation produces more growth. The result is consistent with a role for more complete laws in reducing uncertainty and encouraging specific investments.

Source: More Laws, More Growth? Evidence from U.S. States, working paper, June 5, 2024.
