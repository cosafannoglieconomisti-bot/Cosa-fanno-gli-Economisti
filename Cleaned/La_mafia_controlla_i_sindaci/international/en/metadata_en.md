# Metadati Video - Does the Mafia Control Mayors? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Organized Crime, Captured Politicians, and the Allocation of Public Resources" by Di Cataldo, Mastrorocco, published in The Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization in 2021: What happens when organized crime infiltrates local governments? This study analyzes the impact of collusion between the mafia and politicians in Southern Italy on public finances. The findings show that infiltrated municipalities divert resources toward key sectors for criminal business and are less efficient in tax collection.
