## Descrizione YouTube
The study “Empowering Adolescents to Transform Schools: Lessons from a Behavioral Targeting” by Sule Alan and Elif Kubilay, published in the American Economic Review in 2025, studies a program for disadvantaged middle schools in southeastern Turkey. The intervention entrusts older students, who are socially influential and have high emotional intelligence, with the task of teaching their younger classmates. The experiment shows a reduction in disciplinary problems and antisocial behavior, as well as an increase in the probability of entering selective high schools for the students involved.
