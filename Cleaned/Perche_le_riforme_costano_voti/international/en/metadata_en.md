# Metadati Video - Why Do Reforms Cost Votes? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
Based on the study "Structural Reforms and Elections - Evidence from a World-Wide New Dataset" by Alesina, Furceri, Ostry, Papageorgiou, Quinn, published in the Journal of the European Economic Association in 2024: Is implementing reforms actually worth it for politicians? This monumental study reveals that economic liberalizations are a double-edged sword: the benefits materialize too slowly, turning reforms implemented close to elections into absolute political suicide. However, punishment by voters depends heavily on one key factor: GDP growth.
