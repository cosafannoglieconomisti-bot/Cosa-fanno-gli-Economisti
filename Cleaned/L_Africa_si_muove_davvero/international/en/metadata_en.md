# Metadati Video - Is Africa Truly Moving? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "INTERGENERATIONAL MOBILITY IN AFRICA" by Alesina, Hohmann, Michalopoulos, Papaioannou, published in Econometrica in 2021. Being born in the right place in Africa can radically change your economic destiny. This extraordinary study maps social mobility across 27 African countries, revealing how colonial history and geography influence youth education. A fascinating journey behind the scenes of the so-called 'African miracle'.
