## Descrizione YouTube
The study "Demographics and Automation" by Acemoglu, Restrepo, published in Review of Economic Studies in 2021, reveals how the shortage of middle-aged workers is driving the adoption of industrial robots in rapidly aging countries. A pioneering study uncovers how the demographic evolution of nations like Germany and Japan is the true secret engine of automation. Discover how technology compensates for the declining workforce.
