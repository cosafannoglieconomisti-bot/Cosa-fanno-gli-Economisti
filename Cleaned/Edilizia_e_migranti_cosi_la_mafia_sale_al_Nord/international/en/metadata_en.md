# Video Metadata - Construction and migrants: how the Mafia moved North (EN)

## Description
The study "How Criminal Organizations Expand to Strong States: Local Agreements and Migrant Exploitation in Northern Italy" by Gemma Dipoppa (The Journal of Politics, 2025) explains why Southern Italian mafias manage to take root in the North, where the State is strong, by supplying irregular labor to firms, especially in construction. When demand for informal work grows and migrants arrive from the South, criminal organizations offer local firms workers and an umbrella of illegality. They do not replace a weak State: they sell a useful service precisely where getting caught costs more.
