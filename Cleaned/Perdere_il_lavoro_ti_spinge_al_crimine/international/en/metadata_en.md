# Metadati Video - Does Job Loss Drive You to Crime? (EN)

## Descrizione YouTube
The study "The Effect of Job Loss and Unemployment Insurance on Crime in Brazil" by BRITTO, PINOTTI, SAMPAIO, published in Econometrica in 2022. Does job loss truly push people towards crime? A groundbreaking study from Brazil reveals that unemployment increases the risk of committing crimes by 23%, especially for young and precarious individuals. Discover how unemployment benefits can be a powerful deterrent, but only as long as they last, revealing a direct link between economic stress and law-abiding behavior.
